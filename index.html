How is the Bite Score calculated?

OpenCast blends four signals anglers have relied on for generations: barometric pressure trend (a falling barometer often triggers a pre-front feeding window), wind speed (light-to-moderate chop stirs baitfish without shutting fish down), proximity to dawn/dusk light transitions, cloud cover, and lunar phase (activity tends to cluster near new and full moons). It's a heuristic guide grounded in classic angling & solunar theory — not a guarantee. Fish don't read forecasts.
